AIB had a contract worth hundreds of millions of dollars at stake.

" %} {% set strategy = "

We crafted a path to success for AIB that included a strategic philanthropic program and publicly distinguishing itself from an inappropriately sited data center megaproject that had triggered community opposition.

" %} {% set execution = "
  • Tigercomm immediately sent one of our skilled campaigners to work on the ground 5-6 days a week for a sprint of over two months.
  • We started our work by rapidly, deeply researching community dynamics and needs, as well as understanding the views, goals and pressures on local officials considering the ban.
  • We then helped AIB work with local officials to craft a far-reaching community benefits agreement that reflected input from community members.
