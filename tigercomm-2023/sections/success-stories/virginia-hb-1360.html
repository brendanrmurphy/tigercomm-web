The client needed to pass a Virginia law ending “uneconomic dispatch,” a utility practice that keeps higher-cost power plants running on coal when cheaper renewable energy is available.

The issue for HB 1360 was technical, the legislative window was short and the opposition had a roughly 20-to-1 spending advantage. The client's strongest supporters also lived outside the areas represented by several priority lawmakers. A broad awareness campaign would have squandered limited time and money.

The campaign needed to make a complex issue understandable and reach the people most likely to influence key legislators.

" %} {% set strategy = "

Tigercomm determined that precision outweighed scale.

We turned “uneconomic dispatch” into a clear affordability and accountability message: “Stop Overpriced Power.” The campaign focused on unnecessary costs and consumer fairness without getting lost in utility jargon.

We then built a targeted campaign around six priority lawmakers. Rather than advertise broadly, we reached people in their personal and professional networks, including staff, major donors, former colleagues and local media.

Tigercomm aligned the message, targeting, advertising content, timing and campaign partners behind one political strategy.

" %} {% set execution = "

" %} {% set results = "
