{% module "service_process_details" path="@hubspot/rich_text", label="Process details", html="

Our work generally moves through six stages:

  1. Community research — assess local politics, relationships, routes of influence, community sentiment and potential sources of opposition.
  2. Influence mapping — understand the people, organizations and decision-makers who can shape critical approval milestones.
  3. Leverage assessment — determine which relationships, messages, validators and public actions can move opinion and votes.
  4. Success-path design — map the sequence of stakeholders and decisions that must move for the project to win approval.
  5. Campaign planning — build a coordinated plan with localized messaging, content, outreach tactics and milestone-based goals.
  6. Execution and iteration — carry out the plan, measure progress and continually adjust as political conditions change.
" %}
